# Code of Conduct

EpochLex is an open-source reference project built around shared research, careful editorial judgment, software development, design, testing, and documentation. We want contributors with different backgrounds and skill levels to be able to participate constructively.

## Our standard

Examples of behavior that help create a healthy community include:

- treating other contributors with respect and patience;
- welcoming questions and good-faith disagreement;
- giving feedback about the work rather than attacking the person;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes when evidence changes;
- respecting differences in experience, background, communication style, and technical ability;
- being transparent about conflicts of interest and material AI assistance when relevant to a contribution;
- helping keep discussions focused on improving EpochLex.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- discriminatory or demeaning comments;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- deliberate disruption, trolling, or repeated bad-faith argument;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- knowingly submitting fabricated sources, citations, research claims, or other deceptive material;
- other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in EpochLex repository spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project-managed community channels. It also applies when someone is officially representing EpochLex in a public space.

## Editorial disagreement

EpochLex deals with terminology whose meaning, origin, usage, or status can sometimes be disputed. Good-faith disagreement is welcome. Contributors should support factual claims with appropriate sources, distinguish evidence from interpretation, and preserve uncertainty when the record is unclear.

Disagreement about a definition, source, categorization, or historical claim is not itself a conduct problem. The expectation is that the discussion remains evidence-based and respectful.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, discussions, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Reports of conduct concerns should be made privately to the project maintainer rather than argued publicly in the affected thread. Until a dedicated project contact method is published, use the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Maintainers should handle reports as privately and fairly as reasonably possible and avoid unnecessary disclosure of the reporter's identity.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is informed by common open-source community conduct standards, including the Contributor Covenant, and adapted for EpochLex's editorial and research context.
