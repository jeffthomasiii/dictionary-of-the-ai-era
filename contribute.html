Contribute

AILex is intended to grow through evidence, careful editorial judgment, and contributions from people who encounter AI language in the real world.

What can be contributed?

Useful contributions include proposed new terms, better definitions, corrections, aliases, pronunciation guidance, evidence of early usage, source material, and context showing how a term is being used.

What should a proposed term include?

  • The term and pronunciation.
  • A plain-English definition.
  • A natural use-in-a-sentence example.
  • A suggested category and status.
  • Known aliases or acronyms.
  • Evidence that the term is actually in use.
  • Credible sources when a term is emerging, historical, contested, or attributed to a particular person.

AI-assisted contributions

AI-assisted research and drafting are welcome, but AI output is not treated as evidence. Contributors remain responsible for verifying factual claims, checking sources, and ensuring they have the right to submit the material.

How contributions work today

For now, contributions are handled through the GitHub repository and its contribution guidelines. A friendlier submission workflow can be added later as AILex matures.
