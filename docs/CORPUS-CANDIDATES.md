# EpochLex Corpus Candidates

This document is the source-controlled inventory of **unpublished candidate entries that may merit future review for inclusion in EpochLex**.

It is a planning log, not a publication queue. Appearance here does **not** mean that an entry has been approved, that research is complete, or that the candidate must become a separate entry. Research may show that a candidate should become an entry, be represented as an alias, remain under observation, or not be included.

EpochLex's established editorial and provenance standards still apply. Candidate terms should have documented real-world usage and distinct reader value; meaning evidence should be kept separate from origin evidence; uncertainty should be preserved; and final inclusion and wording require human review.

## How this inventory fits the workflow

- **This file** is the durable repository log for future corpus candidates.
- **GitHub Discussions and term-suggestion issues** are intake surfaces for new ideas and terminology conversations.
- **Issue #58** is an upstream contributor task for identifying high-value coverage gaps.
- **Individual `Research candidate entry` issues** are created only when a candidate is sufficiently actionable for focused contributor research.
- **The GitHub Project** tracks actionable work once a candidate has been promoted into an issue.
- **`data/terms.json` and `data/provenance.json`** remain the source of truth for published entries and their provenance.

## Inventory notes

The initial inventory below was seeded from the September 2026 EpochLex corpus-expansion planning matrix. It contains two planning pools: **repository roadmap candidates** already identified in repository corpus-planning material and a broader **research pool** requiring triage before contributor-ready work.

These planning pools are not new editorial lifecycle states. Candidates with an active research issue are linked directly to that issue.

**Current unpublished inventory: 733 candidates.** The inventory combines the original repository-roadmap and research pools with later audit expansions.

## Adding or promoting candidates

New candidates can enter through repository corpus review, Discussions, contributor term suggestions, or other documented editorial research. Adding a term here records that it may be worth future review; it does not establish inclusion.

When a candidate is ready for focused research, create an individual **Research candidate entry** issue using the repository issue template and link the candidate here. Once research and human editorial review produce a publication decision, update this inventory so it does not drift from the actionable backlog or published corpus.
