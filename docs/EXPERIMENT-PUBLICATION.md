## Version 1.0 evidence boundaries

The publication preserves the report's stated limits:

- repository throughput is not converted into a productivity multiplier;
- no specific labor savings are claimed because historical human effort was not tracked systematically;
- no lifetime token total is reported because the retained evidence does not support a defensible reconstruction;
- exact historical model/configuration is left unspecified where it cannot be verified;
- EpochLex is described as human-directed, AI-assisted, and human-reviewed rather than autonomous;
- findings from one static public reference product are not automatically generalized to substantially different projects, teams, or risk environments.
