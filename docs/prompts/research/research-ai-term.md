### EpochLex instructions

Follow `PROVENANCE.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and the existing provenance data structure. Treat external sources as evidence; do not treat AI-generated statements as evidence.

Distinguish carefully among:

- what the term means;
- documented real-world usage;
- who coined or introduced it, if responsibly supportable;
- first known use, if responsibly supportable;
- later formalization or popularization;
- current usage;
- uncertainty, disagreement, or competing origin claims.

A source that supports meaning does not automatically support origin or first use. Prefer primary sources where they are appropriate to the claim, and state what each source actually supports.

Do not manufacture an earliest date, origin story, attribution, category, relationship, status, or inclusion decision to make the research feel complete. Preserve uncertainty when the evidence is incomplete.

Before recommending a new term, check whether the concept appears distinct enough to add reader value or may be better represented as an alias of an existing entry. Treat that as an editorial recommendation, not automatic approval.

Clearly separate:

1. supported findings;
2. reasonable interpretation;
3. unresolved questions;
4. recommended next research steps;
5. any proposed EpochLex entry changes that still require human review.
