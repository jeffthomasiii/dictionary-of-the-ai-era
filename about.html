Dictionary of the AI Era

About AILex

AILex /A-I-lex/ is a living dictionary of the vocabulary developing around artificial intelligence as technology changes how people work, create, communicate, and think.

What the name means

AILex combines AI with lexicon. It is pronounced /A-I-lex/, saying the letters A and I followed by “lex.” Dictionary of the AI Era is the project descriptor and explains the broader purpose behind the name.

Why this exists

AI is producing more than new software. It is producing new language. Some terms begin as research vocabulary, some emerge in workplaces, some become internet slang, and some describe behaviors that did not meaningfully exist before widespread AI adoption. AILex is intended to collect those terms and explain them in plain English while their meanings are still evolving.

A living reference

The dictionary is intentionally not treated as finished. Terms can move from emerging slang to established language, definitions can become more precise, and new evidence can change how a term's history is understood. Entries are expected to evolve as usage evolves.

Yes, this project is vibe coded.

AI is not only something AILex documents; it is part of how AILex itself is built. The development model is Vibe coded · Human-directed · AI-assisted · Human-reviewed. AI may assist with architecture, code, research drafts, documentation, candidate terms, and definitions, while human editorial judgment governs what is accepted and published.
