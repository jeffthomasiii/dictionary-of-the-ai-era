Dictionary of the AI Era

About AILex

AILex /A-I-lex/ is a living dictionary of the vocabulary developing around artificial intelligence as technology changes how people work, create, communicate, and think.

What the name means

AILex combines AI with lexicon. Dictionary of the AI Era is the project descriptor and explains the broader purpose behind the name.

/A-I-lex/Say the letters A and I, followed by “lex.”

Why this exists

AI is producing more than new software. It is producing new language. Some terms begin as research vocabulary, some emerge in workplaces, some become internet slang, and some describe behaviors that did not meaningfully exist before widespread AI adoption. AILex collects those terms and explains them in plain English while their meanings are still evolving.

A living reference

The dictionary is intentionally not treated as finished. Terms can move from emerging slang to established language, definitions can become more precise, and new evidence can change how a term's history is understood. Entries are expected to evolve as usage evolves.

The public site is the AILex Living Dictionary, which remains current as entries change. AILex also uses named annual editions as frozen historical snapshots for citation and comparison. The first planned snapshot is AILex 2026; it will not be considered released until the annual editorial cutoff is complete and an immutable edition tag and release are created.

How entries are built

Every published term includes a reader-facing definition and example plus a researched provenance record. AILex separates evidence for what a term means from evidence about where it came from, who introduced or popularized it, and what can responsibly be said about its earliest use.

Yes, this project is vibe coded.

AI is not only something AILex documents; it is part of how AILex itself is built. The development model is Vibe coded · Human-directed · AI-assisted · Human-reviewed. AI may assist with architecture, code, research drafts, documentation, candidate terms, and definitions, while human editorial judgment governs what is accepted and published.
